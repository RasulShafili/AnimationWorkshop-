## Formula (both hits are the same story, beat for beat)
1. Line 1 = a strange ACTION plus a stake ("He put a bronze ball in a dragon's mouth and told the emperor: if this ever
   falls, run." / "He saw gaps into a railroad every 39 feet.").
2. "You see, …": context in a single sentence (a race, an emperor, a canyon).
3. Everyone laughs at or mocks the lone inventor or engineer. The rival "wins" first.
4. Escalation with small concrete signs (one ball falls; the gaps shrink: coin → paper; May → July).
5. Payoff: disaster for the doubters. The real science comes in only at the very end, in 2–3 lines
   (seismoscope pendulum; thermal expansion). They end abruptly on the payoff. No CTA, and it loops.
- Real history or engineering facts told as an underdog story, not as a list of facts. Titles are a mystery
  ("Why X Don't Work", "The X That Y"), not "what if".
- 65 s long, VO about 200–220 wpm (fast), dialogue lines quoted inside the narration.
