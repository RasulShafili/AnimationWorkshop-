Owner 2026-11-19 (3D toon, thoughts video): "qorxunc görünməsin" — characters must never look creepy; and "kənarda
zəif qrafikli olsa da detallar var olsun" — background/edge graphics can be simple, but every set needs details.

**Why:** empty rooms looked cheap; staring flat faces and dark night faces can read as creepy to kids' audience.
**How to apply:** default face = soft smile, eye highlights always on, blush on, no wide-eye stare unless the line is
"surprised/scared" and only briefly; faces stay warm-lit at night. Dress every set with cheap low-poly clutter (shelves
with books/jars, posters, plants, rugs, bags, lamps, wall clocks, fridge magnets) — DOF blur hides the low detail.
QA each still for "would a 10-year-old find this face creepy?". Related: [[toon3d-blender-pipeline]], [[lowpoly-premium-style]].
