On 2026-11-26 the owner sent 25 screenshots of errors in "What If Every Adult Disappeared?" after I had reported the video fixed: a car door hanging in the air, a mug handle not touching the mug, two blankets on the bed, kids with 3–4 arms, wheels and pedals turning backwards, a deer that does not look like a deer, babies that look like balls, an unrealistic power plant, and many empty grey or green backgrounds. They asked me to learn this as a skill.

**Why:** my overlap-only QA passed frames that look cheap or wrong to a viewer. The owner judges realism and richness, not only draw order.

**How to apply:**
- Before saying "fixed" or "done", read every sheet asking four questions:
  1. Does each object look like the real thing?
  2. Does anything float or come apart?
  3. Is anything drawn twice, or turning the wrong way?
  4. Is the background empty?
- Every set needs foreground, middle and background detail.
- The concrete rules are in the cartoon-longform SKILL.md, section "Owner's 25-screenshot review". See also [[scale-aim-selfcheck]] and [[two-point-five-d-engine]].
