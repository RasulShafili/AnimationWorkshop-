On 2026-11-16 (ice-age long video) the owner wrote "persanajların hamısı zənci olmasın, ağ adamlarda olsun".

**Why:** the owner wants viewers of every background to see themselves; an all-dark cast felt one-sided to them.

**How to apply:** in every cartoon video, give the hero and several side characters light skin and keep a few
natural browns; never use near-black skin hex values (they render as a black blob). Check each outfit variant's
`skin` field. Related: [[longform-approved-method]], [[longform-cartoon-format]].
