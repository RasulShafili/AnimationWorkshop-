On 2026-11-26 the owner stopped a teaser post and asked for two changes:
- The teaser must start with something interesting that pulls the viewer in.
- The end must not be silent. Instead, a voice should say something like "you can watch the full video on the channel".

**Why:** a quiet frozen end card loses viewers and does not send them to the long video. A slow first second loses the scroll.

**How to apply:**
- Each teaser opens with a 2–3 s cold open: the loudest or most surprising moment, with its own audio. The story then plays from its start.
- The end is a narrator line in the video's own voice (Chatterbox): a question about what happens next, then "Watch the full video on our channel!"
- The end card lasts only as long as that line.
- Normalize loudness to about −14 LUFS.
