On 2026-11-12 the user asked for LONG YouTube "what if" videos in a cartoon-animation style ("2D but not simple 2D"),
pointing to Ifverse's "What If Your Reaction Time Was 10x Faster?" (youtu.be/MroF1XmHOZw, 7:13, 4.7M views) as the
target look and format. They said even 3–5 hours of work is fine as long as the graphics match that quality.

**Reference recipe (analysed):** action in second 1, title question at ~0:30, second person "you", escalating stakes
with a new wow every 60–90 s (chapters), a real fact as a twist, a cost, an emotional moral choice at the climax,
a cliffhanger into Part 2, comment question + subscribe. Look: 16:9, silhouette characters with expressive eyes,
moody dusk lighting, rim light, layered detailed backgrounds, glow/blur/vignette, moving camera, music bed.

**How to apply:** same genre and quality, but OUR OWN characters and stories (never copy Ifverse's red-hoodie hero).
First project: `longform/hearing-100x` ("What If Your Hearing Was 100x Better?"), script in SCRIPT.md, awaiting
approval before production. Related: [[whatif-3d-format]], [[outliers-and-captions]].
