Long-form cartoon stories must move forward like a film: logical cause → effect, action, no "REAL SCIENCE" fact card
in every chapter. Explain things inside the story (a character says it) instead of stopping for a fact insert.
Every shot must show exactly what the narration says (props in hand, right place, right time of day).

**Why:** owner 2026-11-17 on the AI-takeover cut: "errors in every scene and frame, the story is illogical" and
"don't put science facts in the middle every time — the story should progress, logical and action-packed".
**How to apply:** before building shots, check the plot for holes (how did they get there? why is it broken?);
QA every shot against its script line, not only framing. Supersedes the "fact card in every chapter" item of the
cartoon-longform skill recipe. Related: [[longform-approved-method]], [[longform-cartoon-format]].
