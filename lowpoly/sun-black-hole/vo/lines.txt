What if the Sun became a black hole?
Earth wouldn't fall in. Same mass, same pull. Our orbit stays the same.
For eight minutes, nothing changes. The Sun's last light is still on its way.
Then the sky goes black. Stars at noon. Every city light switches on.
Within a week, the plants die. It's minus eighteen.
In a year, minus seventy three. The oceans freeze from the top down.
Life might survive near deep sea vents. Would you? Tag a friend.
