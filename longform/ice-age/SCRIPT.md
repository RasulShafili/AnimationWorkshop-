# What If You Woke Up 30,000 Years Ago?

Format: 16:9 long-form, target ~9:00, second person, day-by-day survival (outlier: "What If You Woke Up 20,000 Years
Ago?" 1.6M = 696× its channel median; "...in Ancient Rome?" 40×). Our own story, characters and science facts.
Engine: copy of longform/zombie-us (Player timeline, Rig2, Chatterbox, strict 640px QA, single-mux assembly).
No on-screen chapter cards, no day stamps that look like chapter titles (owner 2026-11-16) — days are said by the
narrator; a small diegetic tally (scratches on a bone) may show the day count instead.

## Facts & sources
1. Frostbite time vs wind chill (≈ −28 °C wind chill → frostbite in ~30 min) — US National Weather Service wind-chill chart.
2. Snow-cave insulation (interior near 0 °C with −30 °C outside) — survival manuals (US Army FM 3-05.70) / snow science.
3. Ice sheets up to ~3 km thick; sea level up to ~120 m lower at the Last Glacial Maximum; Doggerland land bridge — Clark et al. 2009 (Science); UK Natural History Museum.
4. Woolly mammoth mass up to ~6 t; cave lions (Panthera spelaea) ~10 % larger than modern lions — natural history museums.
5. Fire from flint + pyrite; use-wear on Neanderthal handaxes ~50,000 years old — Sorensen, Claud & Soressi 2018, Scientific Reports.
6. Language change: Old English (~1,000 years ago) is unintelligible to modern readers — standard historical linguistics.
7. Upper Palaeolithic population of Western Europe in the low thousands — Maier et al. 2016 / Bocquet-Appel et al. 2005.
8. Disease mortality after 1492 (up to ~90 % in some regions) — Koch et al. 2019, Quaternary Science Reviews; Crosby, *The Columbian Exchange*.
9. Spear-thrower (atlatl) speeds ~150 km/h; earliest finds ~17,000–20,000 years ago (Solutrean / Magdalenian) — archaeology of projectile weapons.
10. Healed fractures in Palaeolithic skeletons as evidence of care — e.g. Shanidar 1; Spikins et al. 2018 (World Archaeology).
11. Cave paintings in France older than 30,000 years — Chauvet-Pont d'Arc (~36,000 cal BP), UNESCO / Quiles et al. 2016 (PNAS).
