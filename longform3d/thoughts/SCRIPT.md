# What If You Could Hear Everyone's Thoughts?

Format: 16:9 long-form, 3D toon (longform3d/lib3d.py, toon look §12), target 8:30+ (≈1,400 spoken words),
second person ("you" = Leo), Day 1 → Day 30. Simple Ifology-style animation: held poses, static cameras, cuts on key
words, mouth swaps (no lip sync). Owner 2026-11-19: interesting, everyday human emotions, someone secretly liking
someone — **no sexual content at all** (kids' crushes = shy smiles, notes, sitting together).
Demand (outliers.py 2026-11-19): IFNation "What If Everyone Could Hear Your Thoughts?" 1.1M (21.6× median),
Suddenly You "What If Your Crush Could Hear Your Thoughts?" 13×, "…Locked in School for 30 Days With Your Crush" 40×,
IFNation "…Hear What Everyone Says Behind Your Back" 6.4×. Our angle = the reverse (YOU hear THEM), own cast, own rules.

## Logline
A 14-year-old wakes up hearing the thoughts of anyone whose eyes he meets. It starts as the funniest superpower ever,
turns into a crush disaster, a secret about the school's toughest kid, and one thought that saves his best friend's
life — and on Day 30 he learns the one thing the power could never give him.

## Theme
Everyone is fighting a battle you can't hear. And most people aren't thinking about you at all — they're thinking about
themselves (real: the spotlight effect). Final line: "You don't need to read minds. Sometimes you just have to ask."

## Cast (all original; mixed skin tones, light-skinned hero; NO hoodies-with-drawstrings / black heads)
- **YOU = LEO** (14) — light skin, freckles, ginger spiky hair, green sweater, jeans. Signature colour green.
- **MIA** (9) — Leo's little sister, warm brown skin, long black hair, purple sweater. Cookie thief. Secretly lonely.
- **MOM** — light-olive skin, brown bun, mustard cardigan. **DAD** — light skin, beard, blue shirt, job-interview nerves.
- **THEO** (14) — best friend, dark-brown skin (not near-black), short curls, orange jacket. Loyal, jokes when nervous.
- **NORA** (14) — the crush; light-olive skin, dark ponytail, teal sweater, red headband. Draws comics.
- **DEX** (15) — the "tough kid"; tan skin, buzz cut, black jacket. Acts scary; is scared.
- **MR. HALE** — teacher, bald, glasses, brown vest. Hasn't graded the test.

## How a thought looks and sounds (logic rules for every shot)
- The thinker's MOUTH STAYS CLOSED; a soft ring of light pulses around their head; their own voice, with a light echo.
- Leo only hears someone while their eyes meet (Rule 1), within ~3 m (Rule 2); strong feelings are LOUD (Rule 3).
- Every thought shot = Leo's eyes + the thinker's eyes in the same shot or a matched over-the-shoulder pair.
- Day numbers are spoken by the narrator and shown IN the world (fridge calendar, phone lock screen, board date).

## The rules (Leo writes them on sticky notes on his bedroom mirror — our own device)
1. **EYES** — only if our eyes meet. 2. **THREE STEPS** — farther than three steps: silence.
3. **LOUD FEELINGS ARE LOUD** — calm people whisper; scared or angry people shout.

## Real facts used (keep for the description; shown only as in-world signs)
- ~6,200 thought transitions/day — Tseng & Poppenk, Queen's University, Nature Communications (2020).
- Spotlight effect — Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky, Cornell (2000): T-shirt wearers guessed ~50% noticed, ~25% did.
- Semantic decoder — Tang, LeBel, Jain & Huth, UT Austin, Nature Neuroscience (2023): ~16 h of fMRI per person,
  recovered the gist of heard stories, failed when the person resisted (thought of other things).
