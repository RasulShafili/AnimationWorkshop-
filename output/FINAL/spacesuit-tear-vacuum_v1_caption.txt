What actually happens if your spacesuit tears in deep space? 👨‍🚀🌌

Contrary to Hollywood movies, you do not instantly explode! Your skin and circulatory system maintain enough internal pressure to keep you intact. But in the vacuum of space, moisture on your tongue and eyes boils away, air expands violently in your lungs, and oxygen leaves your blood in reverse—leading to hypoxia in just 14 seconds.

Yet, if you are repressurized in an airlock within 90 seconds, you can make a full recovery!
