# nose-hum-test (18 s, 540 frames)
New look on purpose: a theatre stage (art5.ts stage()), a red-hoodie outfit (HOODIE suit), an x-ray light table for the cross-sections, and a gold "stage shutter" wipe between scenes instead of the dither dissolve. The last 26 frames zoom back into frame 0 so the reel loops.
Fact: when you hum with your mouth closed, the air leaves through your nose. Pinch your nose as well and the air has nowhere to go. Pressure above the vocal folds rises until it matches the pressure from the lungs, so the hum dies after about 1–2 s.
Sound: SFX only (tools/build_sfx.py). The hum is a single held pitch. Two-pass loudnorm gives -14.3 LUFS. No silence, no freeze, no black frames.
