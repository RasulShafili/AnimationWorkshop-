# dog-red-ball (2D pixel, 18 s, 540 frames)
Format: pixel-calm-music (see cat-no-sweet v2). On-screen text is English. No voice.
- New look: a sunny park (art8.ts), a golden retriever with run/sniff/wag poses, the owner in a blue jacket, a slide transition.
- Dog-vision filter in scenes.ts draw(): every colour lands on a blue <-> yellow axis with brightness kept. Used as a sweep (hook), a split screen through the ball, and full screen for the blue-ball throw.
- Music: original calm tune in G (G–Em–C–D–G–Bm–C–D–G), music bus 0.55 under the SFX; alimiter after loudnorm because the AAC peak was -0.5 dBFS.
Facts: dogs are dichromats (two cone types, peaks near 429 nm and 555 nm; Neitz et al. 1989). They see blue and yellow; red and green look like dull yellow-brown, so a red toy on grass has little colour contrast. They can still find it by brightness, motion and smell, so the hook says "can't see this ball" in the colour sense, and the caption says "almost disappears".
